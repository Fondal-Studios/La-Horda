# Nigromante — coloso de carne cosida
Generado con imagegen integrado (sin CLI). Fuente: art-source/nigromante-coloso/source.png.

Prompt: Production sprite sheet for La Horda dark fantasy 2D pixel art. Gigantic Frankenstein-inspired stitched flesh golem, necromancer ultimate transformation. Exactly 12 full-body poses in 4 columns × 3 rows, transparent background. Massive mismatched fists, hunched shoulders, square head, muted sage and mauve stitched skin, black sutures, neck bolts, dark purple waist cloth, iron cuffs and chains, green eyes and chest core. Crisp pixel clusters and dark outline. Front, right profile and back: idle, two walk poses, attack. No organs, blood, text, scenery, wings or horns. Same design and scale throughout. Left direction mirrors profile.

El importador separa los 12 componentes, conserva sus píxeles y escala, limpia alfa y alinea los pies. PNG y WebP lossless equivalentes. El cambio es cosmético: no modifica daño, vida, duración, colisión ni restauración del ejército.
